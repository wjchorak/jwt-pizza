# Incident Report 04/03/2026

## Summary

Between the times of 18:45 and 20:50 UTC on 04/03/2026, all users on the platform encountered difficulties in placing pizza orders. The event was triggered by a planned chaos test which disrupted the functionality of calls to the third-party Pizza Factory service.

When attempting to place orders, the factory returned an error indicating the current state of pandemonium with a link to complete the test. The event was detected through Grafana OnCall alerts indicating the presence of logged errors and failed orders. I worked to solve the issue through checking logs (skimmed a little too fast), checking AWS metrics, attempting a few fixes on AWS services, and rereading the logs at the prompting of Professor Jensen. The event had a severe impact on the functionality of the website and prevented 100% of users from purchasing our product.

## Detection

This incident was detected with Grafana OnCall alerts for log errors and failed pizza orders. I received an email notification and responded immediately. After working on the issue with little success, I escalated the issue to Professor Jensen for assistance.

While useful in conjunction with the failed orders alert, the threshold for log errors is currently set too low and sends alerts every time an error occurs. This alert will be modified to only activate if the number of errors exceeds the average or if the errors generated are irregular in some other way (same linked user, quick succession, certain unhandled exceptions, etc.).

## Impact

For 2 hours and 5 minutes between 18:45 and 20:50 MDT on 04/03/2026, any users attempting to purchase a pizza would be unable due to the factory failure.

While no customers were active during the time, 100% would have been affected. Although the traffic simulation script was running on my computer for the duration of the incident, none of the requests generated visible metrics on the website. I will have to perform further testing to identify whether this is the result of an issue with the script, the metrics generation code, or my Grafana dashboard.

## Response

## Resolution

A detailed account of steps taken to resolve the issue is visible in the timeline. The only required fix was to click the link returned in the bad responses, though I also made some attempted fixes in AWS.

## Prevention

The best way to avoid this issue in the future is to avoid pressing the "Initiate Chaos" button, and the best way to improve a future response would be for me to slow down and be more thorough in reading the logs.
